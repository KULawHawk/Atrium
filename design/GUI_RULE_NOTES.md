## Note 1: bounded group confirm for pattern-backed clusters (§5)

**Principle qualified.** §5 lists "batch accept on flagged items without per-item review" as a forbidden anti-pattern.

**Note.** A review screen may offer **one confirm for a cluster of look-alike items** only when every condition below holds. Outside these conditions §5 applies unchanged.

1. The cluster is built on a **known (Canon) pattern with a real scope test**. A cluster grouped by fingerprint alone, with no known pattern, gets one question per item.
2. The confirm covers **ordinary members**. **High-stakes** members (cutoffs, norm tables, scoring rules, decision-tree branches, validity indicators, conversion algorithms, cross-reference resolutions) may be grouped too, but differently: **every high-stakes member of the group is shown** (not only examples), each with **its own tick box and its own text box**. Each tick box **starts unticked** and each text box **starts empty**; the reviewer ticks or types. The ticked members are confirmed together with the group's ruling; two or more ticked is a valid group (for example, 8 of 11 ticked are confirmed). **An unticked member counts as "not the same"**: if the reviewer typed its correct text, that text is recorded as its correction and it is not shown again; if the text box is left empty (spaces only counts as empty), it is presented again on its own. If nothing is ticked, there is no group confirm for the high-stakes members, and each follows the unticked rule. Members confirmed by tick box or corrected by typed text in the group are recorded that way, marked high-stakes, and kept out of accuracy numbers. The tick and text boxes are a setting that ships **off**, with an info icon, turned on only by Jake. **In Clinical mode there are no tick or text boxes**: each high-stakes member keeps the two-reviewer blind check, one at a time. Pre-filling a high-stakes answer (showing a value as already chosen) is a different thing; it stays a separate switch that ships **off** and is turned on only by Jake. *(Amended by Decisions 207 and 212, 2026-11-19. Three points stay open until Jake answers them, and this note does not merge before then: (a) exactly one member ticked; (b) whether typed text is kept out of accuracy numbers, the draft default above, or counted like a one-at-a-time correction; (c) whether typed high-stakes text is read back once.)*
3. Each unshown member is covered only if it **passes the pattern's scope test on its own**. A member that fails becomes its own question. A member the reviewer marks "not the same" is split off and asked again.
4. The reviewer sees the examples the program is **least confident about first, then the most different**. The same item is never shown twice, exact duplicates are shown once with their count, and every shown item carries its confidence number. The confirm is offered only when **at least 3 distinct examples** were shown (**at least 2** when the group holds high-stakes members, Decision 212) (until the member's uniform audit sample is live) and every example got the same ruling.
5. **No answer is pre-selected** on the screen (the never-nudge rules stay whole). Members accepted through a group confirm are recorded as such and are kept out of accuracy numbers.
